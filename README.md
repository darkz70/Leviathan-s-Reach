### Leviathan's Reach - A Faction-Based Steel & Artifacts RPG Mod

---

Leviathan's Reach is a large-scale RPG mod for Fabric/Forge/NeoForge. Master steel weaponry, pledge allegiance to one of three rival factions, hunt down world bosses, and claim legendary artifacts.
